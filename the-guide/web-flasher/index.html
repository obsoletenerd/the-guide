The Guide : Firmware Flasher

Flashes "The Guide" onto Xteink X3 devices

Flashes The Guide's firmware straight from this page over USB. Works in Chrome/Edge and latest Firefox on desktop (Web Serial isn't available in other browsers, or on mobile).

Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Open this page in Chrome/Edge or latest Firefox on a desktop computer instead. This page needs to be served over HTTPS (or from localhost) before it can flash over USB.
Locked units: some third-party-seller Xteink X3s ship with USB flashing locked from the factory. If the connect step fails or the device isn't recognized, unlock it first via the CrossPoint Reader project's Xteink Unlocker (crosspointreader.com/#unlock-tool), then come back here.

Steps

  1. Plug the X3 into this computer with a USB-C cable.
  2. Click Connect device & flash above.
  3. Pick the correct serial port from the browser's device picker.
  4. Wait for the flash to finish — the device will reboot into the Guide Reader automatically.

Recovery: to go back to stock CrossPoint firmware, use the official flasher at crosspointreader.com/#flash-tools.
